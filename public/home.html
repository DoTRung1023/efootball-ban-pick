STRATEGY TIPS
Ban roles, not names — a missing DMF or AMF breaks formations faster than any star.
Their most versatile player is the best ban — it blocks multiple roles at once.
Target the pivot (DMF/CMF) — removing it forces a complete formation rethink.
If they have one elite GK, ban it — quality replacements are rare in any squad.
23 picks means 23 decisions — treat every bench slot as a weapon, not a filler.
Category caps bite mid-draft — know your pick limits before the timer starts.
